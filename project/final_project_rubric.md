# Final Project Rubric

This rubric will be used to grade final project presentations and reports. Undergrads and grads are graded on slightly different scales, so please make sure you read you relevant section closely.

**Presentation slides should be submitted via email by the midnight before your presentation.**

## Basic categories
These categories apply to both undergrads and grads.

1. **Topic** Extends course material in an interesting and non-trivial way.
2. **Content** Accurate, substantive, and demonstrates real research and thoughtful work.
3. **Slides** Professional, visually appealing, and effective aids for audience understanding.
4. **Delivery** Confident and engaging. Demonstrates rehearsal and prior preparation.
5. **Citations** All sources properly cited on slides (and writeup for grads).
6. **Timing** Presentation is within the 7–10 range (harsher penalty for being under time than over).
7. **Group Balance** Group members speak for roughly equal amounts of time.

### Undergrad point distribution (100 pts)
| Category | Points |
|---|---|
| Topic | 10 |
| Content | 30 |
| Slides | 15 |
| Delivery | 20 |
| Citations | 15 |
| Timing | 5 |
| Group Balance | 5 |

### Grad point distribution (100 pts)
Grads are graded on all of the above categories, plus a written report. Points are redistributed to make room for the report.

| Category | Points |
|---|---|
| Topic | 10 |
| Content | 20 |
| Slides | 10 |
| Delivery | 15 |
| Citations | 10 |
| Timing | 5 |
| Group Balance | 5 |
| Written Report | 25 |

#### Written Report
The written report is evaluated on clarity, organization, evidence, and formatting. Full credit requires a report of at least two pages with properly formatted references and appropriate figures or diagrams.
